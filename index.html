How this was compiled

I read the commit history, README and file list of every repo you pushed since July 2026, plus your recent Claude sessions and threads. Older repos from 2025 and the early-2026 course repos (pandas-100, eda-100, the Tableau and Power BI collections) are learning material rather than builds, so they are left out. The empty fitcheck- repo is a duplicate of FitCheck and is also left out.

Shipped means the repo has a working app, API, live demo or submitted report. Active means real work landed but something is still open (an unmerged branch, a missing link, a deploy). Early stage means a scaffold or a single starter file.
