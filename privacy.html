Privacy Policy

Built Tracker · Last updated October 30, 2026

Built Tracker shows a public log of projects and daily build activity by Guntuku Hariprasad. It does not ask you to sign in and does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The app downloads the tracker data from dot-hpg.github.io and keeps a copy on your device so it works offline. It uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

If the app offers reminder notifications, they are scheduled on your device and are never sent through a server.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/dot-hpg/built-tracker.
